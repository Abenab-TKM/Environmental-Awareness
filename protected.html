Protected Areas in Ethiopia

Ethiopia has many protected areas that conserve wildlife, forests, mountains, grasslands, rivers, lakes, and other important ecosystems. These areas provide safe habitats for wildlife and help protect Ethiopia's unique biodiversity.

Simien Mountains National Park

Famous for dramatic mountains, deep valleys, cliffs, and important wildlife.

Location: Amhara Region

Bale Mountains National Park

A large protected area containing forests, wetlands, grasslands, and high mountains.

Location: Oromia Region

Awash National Park

A protected landscape with savanna, woodland, volcanic areas, waterfalls, and wildlife.

Location: Afar and Oromia Regions

Nech Sar National Park

A beautiful park located between Lakes Abaya and Chamo.

Location: Near Arba Minch

Omo National Park

One of Ethiopia's large protected landscapes with forests, rivers, grasslands, and savanna.

Location: Southwestern Ethiopia

Mago National Park

Contains savanna, woodland, rivers, wetlands, and many wildlife species.

Location: Southwestern Ethiopia

Gambella National Park

Protects wetlands, grasslands, woodlands, and important wildlife habitats.

Location: Gambella Region

Abijatta-Shalla National Park

A Rift Valley protected area famous for lakes and many bird species.

Location: Oromia Region

Yangudi Rassa National Park

An arid protected landscape with desert and semi-desert wildlife habitats.

Location: Afar Region
