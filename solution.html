Planting Trees

Adding new trees and vegetation to forests, urban areas, and degraded lands.

  • Absorbs carbon dioxide from the air and releases oxygen.
  • Prevents soil erosion and reduces landslide/flooding risks.
  • Provides critical habitats and food for wildlife.

Saving Water

Using water efficiently and avoiding unnecessary waste at home and in agriculture.

  • Protects freshwater ecosystems like lakes, rivers, and wetlands.
  • Saves energy needed to treat, pump, and heat water.
  • Ensures a stable water supply during droughts.

Saving Energy

Reducing electricity and fuel consumption through smart daily habits.

  • Decreases the burning of fossil fuels (coal, oil, gas).
  • Cuts down greenhouse gas emissions and air pollution.
  • Lowers monthly household electricity bills.

Reducing Plastic

Minimizing single-use plastics and switching to reusable alternatives.

  • Prevents microplastics from polluting oceans and harming marine life.
  • Reduces plastic contamination in our food chain and drinking water.
  • Lowers toxic emissions released during plastic manufacturing.

Using Public Transportation

Choosing shared transport options (buses, trains) instead of personal cars.

  • Drastically reduces individual carbon footprints.
  • Eases traffic congestion in busy cities.
  • Improves overall air quality by reducing vehicle exhaust.

Recycling

Processing used materials (like plastic, glass, paper, and metal) so they can be manufactured into new products.

Key Benefits:

  • Reduces waste sent to landfills and incinerators.
  • Conserves raw natural resources (timber, water, minerals).
  • Saves large amounts of energy during manufacturing.
