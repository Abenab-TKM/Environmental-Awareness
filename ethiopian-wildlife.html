Ethiopian Wildlife

Ethiopian wildlife is rich and diverse, with unique animals such as the Ethiopian wolf, Gelada baboon, and Walia ibex. Protecting wildlife and natural habitats is important for maintaining biodiversity.

Ethiopian Wildlife

Ethiopia has a remarkable variety of wildlife because of its mountains, forests, grasslands, wetlands, and deserts. Explore some of Ethiopia's amazing wildlife below.

Ethiopian Wolf

The Ethiopian wolf is specially adapted to high-altitude grasslands.

Location: Bale Mountains and Ethiopian Highlands

Walia Ibex

The Walia ibex is a mountain goat with large curved horns.

Location: Simien Mountains

Mountain Nyala

A large Ethiopian antelope living in highland areas.

Location: Bale and Arsi Mountains

Gelada

A grass-eating primate found in the Ethiopian Highlands.

Location: Ethiopian Highlands

African Wild Dog

A highly social predator that lives and hunts in packs.

Location: Suitable habitats in Ethiopia

Black-and-white Colobus

A forest-dwelling monkey with striking black and white fur.

Location: Ethiopian forests

Olive Baboon

An adaptable primate that lives in groups called troops.

Location: Forests and grasslands

African Lion

A powerful predator that plays an important role in ecosystems.

Location: Grasslands and protected areas

African Elephant

The largest land animal and an important ecosystem engineer.

Location: Parks and forests

Hippopotamus

A large animal that spends much of its time in rivers and lakes.

Location: Rivers and lakes

Nile Crocodile

A powerful freshwater reptile living near rivers and lakes.

Location: Wetlands and rivers

Secretary Bird

A tall bird of prey that hunts while walking on the ground.

Location: Grasslands and open habitats
