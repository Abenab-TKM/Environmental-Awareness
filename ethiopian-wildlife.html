Ethiopian Wildlife

Ethiopia has a remarkable variety of wildlife because of its mountains, forests, grasslands, wetlands, and deserts. The country is home to mammals, birds, reptiles, and many other species. Explore some of Ethiopia's amazing wildlife below.

Ethiopian Wolf

The Ethiopian wolf is one of the most famous animals in Ethiopia. It is specially adapted to high-altitude grasslands and feeds mainly on small mammals.

Location: Bale Mountains, Simien Mountains and Ethiopian Highlands

Walia Ibex

The Walia ibex is a mountain goat with large curved horns. It is one of Ethiopia's best-known wildlife species and lives among steep mountain cliffs.

Location: Simien Mountains

Mountain Nyala

The mountain nyala is a large antelope with impressive horns. It lives in Ethiopia's highland forests and grasslands.

Location: Bale and Arsi Mountains

Gelada

The gelada is a grass-eating primate found in the Ethiopian Highlands. It is recognized by the red patch on its chest and its social groups.

Location: Simien Mountains and Ethiopian Highlands

African Wild Dog

The African wild dog is a social predator that lives in groups. It has a distinctive coat pattern and requires large areas of suitable habitat.

Location: Bale Mountains and other suitable areas

Black-and-white Colobus

The black-and-white colobus is a forest-dwelling monkey with striking black and white fur. It spends much of its time in trees.

Location: Ethiopian forests

Olive Baboon

Olive baboons are adaptable primates that live in different environments. They are social animals that live in groups called troops.

Location: Forests, grasslands and savannas of Ethiopia

African Lion

Lions are powerful predators found in parts of Ethiopia. They live in groups and play an important role in maintaining the balance of ecosystems.

Location: Grasslands, savannas and protected areas

African Elephant

African elephants are the largest land animals. They help shape ecosystems by feeding on vegetation and creating pathways through their habitats.

Location: National parks, forests and savanna regions

Hippopotamus

Hippopotamuses spend much of their time in rivers and lakes. They are important parts of freshwater ecosystems in areas where they occur.

Location: Rivers and lakes including areas around Lake Tana

Nile Crocodile

The Nile crocodile is a large freshwater reptile. It is a powerful predator that lives near rivers, lakes, and wetlands.

Location: Rivers, lakes and wetlands of Ethiopia

Secretary Bird

The secretary bird is a tall bird of prey that hunts on the ground. It is recognized by its long legs and distinctive crest of feathers.

Location: Grasslands and open habitats
