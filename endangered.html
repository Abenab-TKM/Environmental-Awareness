Endangered Animals in Ethiopia

Discover the unique wildlife that calls Ethiopia home and learn about the threats they face.

Meet Endangered Animals

Ethiopian red Wolf

The Ethiopian wolf, also called the Ethiopian red fox, is found mainly in Ethiopia’s highlands. About 450 adults remain, making it Africa’s most endangered carnivore. It lives in places such as the Bale and Simien Mountains.

Walia Ibex

The Walia Ibex is an endangered animal found only in Ethiopia’s Simien Mountains. In 2024, only about 306 individuals were counted. Its main threats are poaching and habitat loss.

Mountain Nyala

The Mountain Nyala is an endangered antelope found only in Ethiopia. About 4,000 remain, mainly in the Bale and Arsi Mountains. Its population is threatened by habitat loss and hunting.

wild donkey

The wild donkey is an endangered species found in Ethiopia. It lives in arid regions and is threatened by habitat loss and hunting.

Gelada

The Gelada is found mainly in Ethiopia’s highlands, especially the Simien Mountains. Its population is threatened by habitat loss and human activity.

African Wild Dogs

African Wild Dogs are found in parts of Ethiopia, including the Bale Mountains. Their numbers are declining because of habitat loss and disease.

Swayne’s Hartebeest

Swayne’s Hartebeest is found only in Ethiopia. It lives mainly in protected grasslands and is threatened by habitat loss and hunting.

Black-and-white Colobus

The Black-and-white Colobus lives in Ethiopia’s forests. Its population is threatened by deforestation and loss of habitat.
