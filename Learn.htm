Understanding Our Environment

What it is, why it matters, and our impact.

What is the Environment?

The environment encompasses all living and non-living components that interact within an ecosystem.

  • Air, water, soil, and climate
  • Plants, animals, and microorganisms
  • Natural resources (forests, oceans)

Why is it Important?

A healthy environment is the foundation of life and economy.

Sustains Life

Provides air, water, food.

Climate

Regulates weather patterns.

Biodiversity

Ensures ecosystem stability.

Human Impact

Human activities are significantly altering the planet:

  • Pollution: Air, water, soil contamination.
  • Deforestation: Loss of critical habitats.
  • Climate Change: Rising global temperatures.
  • Overconsumption: Depleting resources.
