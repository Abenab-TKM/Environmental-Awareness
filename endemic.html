Endemic Animals of Ethiopia

Ethiopia is home to many unique animals that are found naturally only in Ethiopia. These endemic species are an important part of Ethiopia's natural heritage and biodiversity. Learn about some of the country's most remarkable endemic animals below.

Ethiopian Wolf

The Ethiopian wolf is one of Ethiopia's most famous endemic mammals.

Location: Bale Mountains, Simien Mountains and Ethiopian Highlands

Walia Ibex

The Walia ibex is a wild mountain goat found only in Ethiopia.

Location: Simien Mountains

Mountain Nyala

The mountain nyala is a large antelope native to Ethiopia.

Location: Bale and Arsi Mountains

Gelada

The gelada is a grass-eating primate found in the Ethiopian Highlands.

Location: Simien Mountains and Ethiopian Highlands

Ethiopian Hare

The Ethiopian hare is associated with Ethiopia's highland environments.

Location: Ethiopian Highlands

Bale Monkey

The Bale monkey is a primate native to Ethiopia.

Location: Bale Mountains

Ethiopian Bush Crow

The Ethiopian bush crow is a unique bird found in southern Ethiopia.

Location: Southern Ethiopia

Rouget's Rail

Rouget's rail is a small bird found in Ethiopian highland habitats.

Location: Ethiopian Highlands

Harwood's Francolin

Harwood's francolin is a rare bird associated with Ethiopia.

Location: Central and northern Ethiopia
