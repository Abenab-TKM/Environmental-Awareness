About Black Bulls

We are students working together to promote environmental awareness and encourage people to protect our planet.

Who We Are

We are the Black Bulls, a group of 2026th INSA Summer Camp students. Our team created this website to increase environmental awareness and help people understand the importance of protecting nature.


This website provides information about environmental problems such as pollution, climate change, waste, deforestation, and the protection of wildlife and natural resources. We believe that small actions can make a big difference for our future.

Our Mission

To educate and inspire people to understand environmental challenges and take positive actions to protect the Earth.

Our Vision

To build a cleaner, healthier, and more sustainable future where everyone takes responsibility for protecting the environment.
