Protected Areas in Ethiopia

Ethiopia has many protected areas that conserve wildlife, forests, mountains, grasslands, rivers, lakes, and other important ecosystems. These areas provide safe habitats for wildlife and help protect Ethiopia's unique biodiversity.

Simien Mountains National Park

Simien Mountains National Park is famous for its dramatic mountains, deep valleys, and cliffs. It protects important wildlife including the Walia ibex, gelada, and Ethiopian wolf.

Location: Amhara Region

Bale Mountains National Park

Bale Mountains National Park contains high-altitude grasslands, wetlands, forests, and the Sanetti Plateau. It is especially important for the conservation of the Ethiopian wolf.

Location: Oromia Region

Awash National Park

Awash National Park contains savanna, acacia woodland, volcanic landscapes, waterfalls, and the Awash River. It supports animals such as oryx, gazelles, kudu, and many bird species.

Location: Afar and Oromia Regions

Nech Sar National Park

Nech Sar is known for its grasslands, wildlife, and beautiful landscapes between Lakes Abaya and Chamo. The park provides habitat for several species of mammals and birds.

Location: Southern Ethiopia, near Arba Minch

Omo National Park

Omo National Park is one of Ethiopia's large protected landscapes. Its forests, grasslands, rivers, and savanna support many species of wildlife.

Location: Southwestern Ethiopia

Mago National Park

Mago National Park contains savanna, woodland, rivers, and wetlands. It provides habitat for many large mammals and other wildlife species.

Location: Southwestern Ethiopia

Gambella National Park

Gambella National Park protects lowland ecosystems including wetlands, woodlands, and grasslands. The region is important for large mammals and migratory wildlife.

Location: Gambella Region

Abijatta-Shalla National Park

Abijatta-Shalla National Park is centered around two Rift Valley lakes. The area is especially important for birds, including large numbers of flamingos.

Location: Oromia Region

Yangudi Rassa National Park

Yangudi Rassa protects an arid landscape in the Afar Region. Its desert and semi-desert habitats provide important space for wildlife adapted to dry environments.

Location: Afar Region
