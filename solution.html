Rcycling

Definition

Recycling is the process of collecting and processing waste materials to create new products, serving as a critical component of the circular economy. The primary uses of recycling include conserving natural resources like timber and minerals, reducing landfill waste, and lowering greenhouse gas emissions by saving energy compared to extracting raw materials. Conserves Natural Resources: Reduces the need to extract raw materials like timber, water, minerals, and oil. For example, recycling paper saves trees and forests.

Uses

  • Saves Energy: Manufacturing products from recycled materials consumes significantly less energy than creating them from raw, virgin materials (e.g., recycling aluminum cans saves up to 95% of the energy needed to make new ones).
  • Reduces Greenhouse Gas Emissions: Lower energy consumption means fewer fossil fuels are burned, cutting down carbon emissions that drive climate change.
  • Protects Wildlife & Ecosystems: Decreases habitat destruction caused by mining, deforestation, and industrial waste.
