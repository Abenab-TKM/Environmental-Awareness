Endemic Animals of Ethiopia

Ethiopia is home to many unique animals that are found naturally only in Ethiopia. These endemic species are an important part of Ethiopia's natural heritage and biodiversity. Learn about some of the country's most remarkable endemic animals below.

Ethiopian Wolf

The Ethiopian wolf is one of Ethiopia's most famous endemic mammals. It is the rarest canid in Africa and is specially adapted to living in the country's high-altitude grasslands.

Location: Bale Mountains, Simien Mountains and Ethiopian Highlands

Walia Ibex

The Walia ibex is a wild mountain goat found only in Ethiopia. It has long curved horns and lives on steep cliffs and rocky mountains.

Location: Simien Mountains

Mountain Nyala

The mountain nyala is a large antelope that is native to Ethiopia. It is known for its impressive horns and lives mainly in highland forests and grasslands.

Location: Bale and Arsi Mountains

Gelada

The gelada is a grass-eating primate found naturally only in the Ethiopian Highlands. It is sometimes called the bleeding-heart monkey because of the red patch on its chest.

Location: Simien Mountains and Ethiopian Highlands

Ethiopian Hare

The Ethiopian hare is a mammal associated with Ethiopia's highland environments. It is adapted to grassland and open habitats and contributes to the local ecosystem.

Location: Ethiopian Highlands

Bale Monkey

The Bale monkey is a primate native to Ethiopia. It is strongly associated with bamboo forests and highland habitats in the Bale Mountains.

Location: Bale Mountains

Ethiopian Bush Crow

The Ethiopian bush crow is a bird found only in southern Ethiopia. It is easily recognized by its pale body, dark wings, and distinctive appearance.

Location: Southern Ethiopia

Rouget's Rail

Rouget's rail is a small bird native to the highlands of Ethiopia and Eritrea. It lives in grasslands, wetlands, and areas with thick vegetation.

Location: Ethiopian Highlands

Harwood's Francolin

Harwood's francolin is a rare bird associated with Ethiopia. It depends on suitable grassland and woodland habitats for food and shelter.

Location: Central and northern Ethiopia
